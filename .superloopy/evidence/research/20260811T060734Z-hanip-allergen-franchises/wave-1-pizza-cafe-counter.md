# Wave 1 — pizza, cafe, bakery, and counter-brief

## Digest

- No candidate is an unconditional adopter in this lane.
- Compose Coffee is structurally closest: stable menu detail URLs and all five displayed nutrient fields plus allergens. It is deferred because pages have no official source date and a live seasonal row is internally impossible (`286.32 g` carbohydrate at `361.10 kcal`).
- A Twosome Place sandwich has a full label, but coverage is inconsistent: another official detail omits total carbohydrate and fat. It cannot support a whole-brand collector.
- Domino's, Pizza Hut, Mr. Pizza, Mega MGC, Paik's Coffee, and Tous les Jours omit one or more fields required by the current project contract or separate the evidence in ways that cannot be joined safely.

## Counter-brief verdict

Do not add a second brand merely to increase breadth. Kyochon has a stronger current official-source chain than Compose because its sampled detail exposes the project's exact displayed fields, serving basis, and allergen mapping without a known internal numeric contradiction. Compose remains a future candidate only after row-level calorie sanity checks and quarantine reporting are implemented.

## EXPAND

- LEAD: Compose full `item_srl` crawl — WHY: strongest alternative source topology — ANGLE: require five fields, explicit allergens, serving unit, and macro-calorie sanity; quarantine failures.
- LEAD: Food Safety Korea franchise database — WHY: could cross-check official numeric anomalies — ANGLE: only if the project later permits a regulator source alongside brand sources.
- DEAD END: Domino's, Pizza Hut, and Mr. Pizza tables — WHY: required fields are structurally incomplete.
- DEAD END: Mega MGC, Paik's Coffee, and Tous les Jours — WHY: total fat/carbohydrate coverage does not meet the proposed stricter schema interpretation.
- DEAD END: Twosome whole-brand collection — WHY: complete label popup coverage varies by product.
